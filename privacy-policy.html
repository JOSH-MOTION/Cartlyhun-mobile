Privacy Policy

Cartlyhubgh Mobile Application

Last updated: June 15, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Cartlyhubgh ("we", "our", or "us"). We operate the Cartlyhubgh mobile application (the "App"), a marketplace platform for buying and selling goods and services in Ghana. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our App.

By using Cartlyhubgh, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you disagree, please discontinue using the App.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, profile photo, and phone number when you register.
  • Listing Information: Product names, descriptions, prices, images, and category data you upload.
  • Location Data: Approximate or precise location when you grant permission, used to show nearby listings.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and push notification tokens.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, search queries, products viewed, and interactions within the App.
  • Payment Information: We do not store full payment card details. Payments are processed securely via Paystack.
  • Communications: Messages exchanged between buyers and sellers through the in-app chat feature.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To display your product listings to other users.
  • To facilitate transactions between buyers and sellers.
  • To send push notifications about orders, messages, and promotions (you can opt out at any time).
  • To improve and personalise your experience in the App.
  • To detect fraud, enforce our Terms of Service, and maintain security.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services that may receive your data:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, database (Firestore), and cloud storage. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — Optional social login. Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudinary — Image hosting and optimisation. Cloudinary Privacy Policy
  • Paystack — Payment processing. Paystack Privacy Policy
  • Expo / EAS — App distribution and over-the-air updates. Expo Privacy Policy

5. Photos and Camera Access

Cartlyhubgh requests access to your camera and photo library solely to allow you to upload images for product listings and your profile photo. We do not access, store, or transmit any photos without your explicit action.

6. Location Data

We request location permission to display your general area on product listings and to surface nearby items. Location data is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings.

7. Push Notifications

We may send push notifications for order updates, new messages, and promotional offers. You can opt out of notifications at any time through your device settings or the in-app notification preferences.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

9. Children's Privacy

Cartlyhubgh is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), Firebase Security Rules, and secure authentication. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

11. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
